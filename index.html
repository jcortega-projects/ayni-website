What is Ayni?

The Host

Hi, I'm Juan Ortega

I'm an Ecuadorian Fulbright scholar and a graduate student in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University. My work sits at the intersection of technology and human connection — I research how machines can offer emotional support without making us less human.

Ayni was born from a simple belief: that the stories, lessons, and wisdom people carry are too valuable to disappear. I started this as a space to capture real conversations with people building, learning, and leading around the world.

Technology should become more human, not the other way around.
